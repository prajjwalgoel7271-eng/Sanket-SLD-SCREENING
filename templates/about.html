📖 About Sanket & Clinical Methodology

Evidence-based digital assessment framework for Specific Learning Disorders.

Project Mission

Sanket was designed as a dedicated, non-invasive digital screening system to identify Specific Learning Disorders (SLD) — including Dyslexia, Dyscalculia, and Dysgraphia — across all developmental stages. By combining objective task performance, latency tracking, keystroke dynamics, and executive distress questionnaires, Sanket delivers precise risk stratification.

Diagnostic Standards & Criteria

Sanket's screening algorithms are constructed in strict alignment with international clinical diagnostic manuals:

  • DSM-5-TR (Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders): Criteria A1 through A6 for 315.00 (Reading), 315.1 (Mathematics), and 315.2 (Written Expression).
  • ICD-11 (International Classification of Diseases): Codes 6A03.0 (Impairment in Reading), 6A03.1 (Impairment in Mathematics), and 6A03.2 (Impairment in Written Expression).

Key Innovations

🎧 Audio Dictated Spelling

Real & pseudo-word dictation testing phonological decoding.

⌨️ Keystroke Pacing Dynamics

Measures pause ratios and character speed decay from the first letter typed.

🛡️ Social Masking Detection

Flags adolescent coping denial when objective risk contrasts low self-reported distress.
