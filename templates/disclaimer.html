⚖️ Medical & Clinical Disclaimer

Important information regarding digital screening tools.

⚠️ Screening Tool vs Medical Diagnosis

Sanket is a digital risk screening tool designed to assist educators, parents, and healthcare professionals in early identification of Specific Learning Disorder risk markers. Sanket is NOT a formal medical diagnosis.

Clinical Recommendations:

  • A formal clinical diagnosis of Dyslexia, Dyscalculia, or Dysgraphia requires a comprehensive psychometric assessment conducted by a licensed Clinical Psychologist, Educational Psychologist, or Neuropsychologist.
  • Screening results produced by Sanket should be shared with qualified clinical specialists to guide further formal evaluation.
  • If high risk is indicated, early educational interventions and accommodations should be explored promptly in collaboration with certified specialists.
